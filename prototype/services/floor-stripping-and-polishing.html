Floor stripping and polishing

Make your floor look new again. Stripping removes old, worn polish, and a new protective coat gives the floor shine and helps it stand up to everyday use.

Request a quote

When does a floor need a new coat?

A floor that looks dull and grey even straight after cleaning, or that has clear walkways, is often ready for stripping and a new coat. You can read more about the signs in our floor maintenance guide.

Floors we treat

Polishing suits vinyl and linoleum, which are common in offices, shops, schools and shared areas. Tiles and wooden floors have their own treatments and are not polished.

How the job is done

  1. The floor is cleared and vacuumed.
  2. Old polish is stripped off with a machine.
  3. The floor is rinsed and left to dry.
  4. New polish is applied in several thin coats.
  5. The floor cures before it is used again.

Area

We treat floors in Haugesund, on Karmøy and across the rest of Haugalandet. Floor treatment can also be combined with specialist cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

Can the work be done outside opening hours?

Because the floor has to dry between coats, the work is often best done in the evening or at the weekend. The timing is agreed with you.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the floor and how many coats are needed. You will get an estimate with the quote.
