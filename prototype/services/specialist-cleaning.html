Specialist cleaning for businesses

Sometimes premises need more than regular cleaning. Specialist cleaning is tailored to the job, whether it is a thorough deep clean or premises that need to be ready for new tenants.

Typical jobs

  • Deep cleaning of offices and shared areas

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between specialist and regular cleaning?

Regular cleaning keeps premises clean from week to week. Specialist cleaning is more thorough and covers what is not done in the regular cleaning.

Can specialist cleaning be booked as a one-off job?

Yes, specialist cleaning can be booked without a regular agreement. Get in touch for a no-obligation quote.
