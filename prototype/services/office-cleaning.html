Office and commercial cleaning in Haugalandet

Daily or regular cleaning of offices, shops and shared areas. You decide how often and what is included, and we make sure it is done thoroughly every time.

Request a quote

What can be included

  • Dusting and wiping desks, shelves and surfaces
  • Vacuuming and washing floors
  • Toilets, changing rooms and showers
  • Kitchens, canteens and break rooms
  • Emptying bins and recycling
  • Entrances, stairs and shared areas

The scope is tailored to your premises. You decide what is included.

You decide how often

Some need cleaning every day, others manage with once a week or every other week. We put together a solution based on how many people use the premises, how many visitors you have and your budget. It can be adjusted along the way.

How to get started

  1. Request a no-obligation quote using the form or by phone.
  2. We go through your premises and needs together.
  3. Cleaning starts as agreed.

Where we provide office cleaning

We are based in Karmsund and take on work in Haugesund, on Karmøy and across the rest of Haugalandet.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to sign up for a long time?

The agreement is tailored to you. Feel free to ask about notice periods and flexibility when you request a quote.

Yes. R & E Renhold AS is approved by the Labour Inspection Authority, as every cleaning company in Norway must be.

How do we find out what it costs?

The price depends on size, how often you want cleaning and what is included. Request a no-obligation quote and you will get a price tailored to your premises.
