Deep cleaning: a simple plan for business and home

Business and homePublished 27 November 20262 min read

Once or twice a year it makes sense to go deeper than your regular cleaning. A deep clean covers everything that does not get done every week. Here is a plan that works both at work and at home.

When is a good time for a deep clean?

Spring is popular, when winter's grit, salt and dust need to go. Many also do a round in autumn or before Christmas. At work, quiet periods such as the summer holidays or the days between Christmas and New Year are often the best time, because the premises are emptier.

Choose the right product for the right dirt

Using a product that suits the dirt saves a lot of time:

Limescale and soap residue

Acidic products, such as descaler, dissolve limescale in showers, sinks and on fittings. Do not use them on natural stone such as marble and limestone.

Grease and food residue

Alkaline products dissolve grease in the kitchen, for example on the extractor fan, cooker and cupboard doors.

Everyday dirt

A mild all-purpose cleaner and a microfibre cloth handle most things.

Important: Never mix bleach with acidic products. It can release toxic gases.

Deep cleaning at work

  • Empty and clean kitchen cupboards, the fridge and the microwave.
  • Clean chairs, sofas and other textiles.
  • Dust high surfaces, lamps and vents.
  • Clean windows, frames and glass walls.
  • Clean keyboards, phones and screens with a suitable product.
  • Tidy storage rooms, archives and store cupboards.
  • Consider stripping and re-polishing worn floors.

The floor often makes the biggest difference. Read more about floor polishing and maintenance.

Deep cleaning at home

  • Wash walls, doors and frames where needed.
  • Clean inside the kitchen cupboards.
  • Clean the oven, extractor fan and grease filter.
  • Wash curtains, cushions and rugs.
  • Descale the bathroom thoroughly, including drains and grout.
  • Vacuum and clean under and behind the furniture.

Moving out? Then you need an even more thorough round. Use our move-out cleaning checklist.

Tips that save time

  • Work from top to bottom, and from the back of the room towards the door.
  • Do one room at a time, so you can see your progress.
  • Let products work for a few minutes before you scrub.
  • Use microfibre, which removes more dirt with less product.
  • Put time in the calendar, so it actually gets done.

Frequently asked questions

How often should you deep clean?

Once or twice a year suits most people. Many choose spring and autumn.

What is the difference between regular cleaning and a deep clean?

Regular cleaning keeps surfaces clean from week to week. A deep clean covers what is done less often, such as inside cupboards, high surfaces, windows and vents.

Can vinegar be used on everything?

No. Vinegar is acidic and can damage natural stone such as marble and limestone, and some metals. Always check what the surface can tolerate.
