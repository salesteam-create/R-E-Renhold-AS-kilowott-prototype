A clean office all week: 8 tips that actually work

BusinessPublished 27 November 20263 min read

A clean office is about more than the cleaner stopping by. Small habits during the week decide whether the premises still feel tidy on Friday afternoon, or only on Monday morning. Here are eight tips we give business customers in Haugesund and on Karmøy.

Contents

  2. 8 tips for a cleaner office

Make a simple cleaning plan

A cleaning plan does not need to be long. One page showing what is done daily, weekly and monthly, and who is responsible, is enough. It helps to split the tasks in two: what employees do themselves, such as washing up and tidying their own desks, and what the cleaner takes care of, such as floors, toilets and surfaces.

8 tips for a cleaner office

1. Clear your desk before you go home

A clear desk can be wiped down. Piles of paper, cups and cables mean the cleaner has to skip those surfaces. Consider a "clear desk" rule for Fridays.

2. Agree on kitchen rules

The kitchen is the area most likely to become a problem. Agree that everyone puts their own cup in the dishwasher, and that the fridge is emptied on a set day.

3. Wipe touch points more often

Door handles, light switches, the coffee machine, printers and lift buttons are touched by many people every day. This is where dirt and germs spread most easily, so these surfaces deserve extra attention.

4. Put down good entrance mats

Much of the dirt on the floor is carried in on shoes. With wet weather and winter grit in Haugalandet, a good mat both outside and inside the door makes a big difference. It protects the floor too.

5. Make recycling easy

Clearly labelled bins in fixed places mean less clutter and fewer overflowing bins on desks.

6. Keep the toilets stocked

Running out of soap or paper is one of the first things employees and visitors notice. Agree who is responsible for refills between cleans.

7. Think about the indoor climate

Dust collects on high surfaces, lamps and vents. Make sure these are wiped regularly, and that the ventilation system is serviced on schedule.

8. Agree on a fixed cleaning frequency

The last tip is the most important one: decide how often the premises should be cleaned, and stick to it.

How often should an office be cleaned?

There is no single answer, but the number of employees, customer visits and type of premises are what decide it. These are common starting points:

  • Few employees and few visitors: often once or twice a week.
  • Many employees or daily customer visits: several times a week or daily.
  • Toilets and kitchens: more often than the rest of the premises, whatever the size.

How often should an office be cleaned?

It depends on how many people work there and how many visitors you have. Many small offices manage with cleaning once or twice a week, while toilets and kitchens often need more.

What does office cleaning cost?

No. Cleaning can often be scheduled at times that do not disturb the working day.
