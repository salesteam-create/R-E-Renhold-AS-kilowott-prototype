What the form is for

Businesses that want to know what cleaning would cost can ask for a price without writing a long email or calling during office hours. They answer a few quick questions, and you receive a request with everything needed to prepare a quote, which simply means a price offer for a specific job.

Good to know: the form does not calculate a price on screen. It collects the details, and you send the price afterwards by phone or email. That is why the heading says "Be om pris på under ett minutt" (request a price in under a minute).

How a visitor uses it

  1. Choose a serviceOffice cleaning, specialist cleaning or floor care. One click, then "Next".
  2. Describe the buildingType of building, location, approximate size and how often cleaning is needed.
  3. Leave contact detailsCompany, name, phone and email, plus an optional comment. Then "Send request".
  4. Check the summaryA thank-you message appears with their answers, so they can see that everything is correct.

A progress bar shows how far they have come, and a "Back" button lets them correct an answer. If a required field is missing or an email address is not valid, the form explains what to fix before it sends.

Why it helps your customers and your business

For your customers

  • Quick. About a minute of clicking instead of writing an email.
  • No obligation. Asking for a price commits them to nothing.
  • Open at any hour. They can send a request in the evening or at the weekend.
  • A clearer answer. Your reply can be specific from the start.

For R & E Renhold

  • More enquiries. Short steps and clickable choices mean fewer visitors give up halfway.
  • Better leads. A lead is a potential customer. You see size, location and frequency straight away.
  • Faster replies. All details arrive in one place, and the first company to reply often wins the job.
  • Measurable. Every request can be counted, so you can see what the website brings in each month.

What "conversion" means

Conversion is the share of visitors who take the step you want, here sending a quote request. The example below shows the idea with round numbers. These are illustrative figures, not a forecast.

20 requests from 1,000 visitors is a 2% conversion rate. A simpler form, a visible quote button on every page and a quick reply all help raise that share.

Possible next step: a price estimate

The form could later show an estimated price range on screen, based on size and frequency. It is eye-catching, but it needs your real price levels, because customers tend to hold you to any figure they see. We recommend starting with the quote form and adding an estimate once your pricing is agreed.

In the prototype nothing is sent. On the live website, requests would arrive in your email inbox or customer system.
