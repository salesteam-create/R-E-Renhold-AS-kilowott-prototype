6 benefits of professional cleaning for your business

BusinessPublished 27 November 20263 min read

Many small businesses start out with employees doing the cleaning themselves. That works for a while, but over time the standard often slips, and some people end up doing more than others. Here are six reasons more businesses outsource their cleaning, and what to check before you choose a company.

1. Your staff can spend their time on their actual jobs

When employees have to clean toilets, empty bins and mop floors, it takes time away from the work they were hired to do. Add up the hours over a year and it quickly becomes more than you would expect. With regular cleaning there are no rotas to hand out, and nobody has to stay behind after work.

2. Consistent quality, week after week

A professional cleaning company works to an agreed plan. That means the same tasks are done every time, including the easy-to-forget ones like skirting boards, door handles and vents. A regular team also gets to know the building and where it needs extra attention.

3. Better indoor climate and wellbeing

Dust and dirt on surfaces and floors affect how the air feels, and how it feels to come to work. Many people find that regular, thorough cleaning means fewer problems with dust and a nicer place to work. It is especially noticeable in open-plan offices where many people share the same surfaces.

4. A good first impression for customers and visitors

The entrance, meeting rooms and toilets are often the first things customers see. Clean, tidy premises say something about how your business works, without you having to say a word.

5. The right equipment and methods

Cleaners use equipment and products made for the job, such as microfibre that removes more dirt with fewer chemicals. The right products also protect your surfaces. A vinyl or linoleum floor can be damaged by the wrong cleaner, while the right care makes it last longer. Read more in our guide to floor polishing and maintenance.

6. Predictable costs

With a fixed agreement you know what cleaning costs every month. There is no equipment or products to buy, and no need to find cover when someone is ill or on holiday.

Check that the cleaning company is approved

In Norway, every company that sells cleaning services must be approved by the Labour Inspection Authority (Arbeidstilsynet) and listed in its register of approved cleaning companies. The scheme is there to ensure fair pay and working conditions in the industry. It is not legal to buy cleaning from a company that is not approved, and businesses that buy cleaning services are required to check this.

  • Look the company up in the Labour Inspection Authority's register of approved cleaning companies.
  • Ask to see the cleaners' HMS cards. Everyone working in cleaning must have one.
  • Ask who your contact person is, and how to report anything that is not as agreed.

What should a cleaning agreement include?

A good agreement makes life easier for both sides. Make sure it describes:

  • which tasks are included, and how often
  • what time of day the cleaning takes place
  • who your regular contact person is
  • how issues and requests for changes are handled
  • the price, notice period and what is included

Frequently asked questions

How do I check whether a cleaning company is approved?

Look the company up in the Labour Inspection Authority's register of approved cleaning companies. Cleaners must also carry an HMS card while at work.

Is professional cleaning worth it for a small business?

Often, yes, especially when you count the time employees would otherwise spend cleaning. Many small businesses start with cleaning once a week.

Can we combine our own cleaning with a professional service?

Yes. Many let employees handle simple daily tasks, such as washing up and tidying their own desks, while the cleaning company does the rest.
