Commercial cleaning on Karmøy

Karmøy lies just across the Karmsund strait from us. We offer cleaning to businesses on the island, whether your premises are in Kopervik, Åkrehamn, Avaldsnes or elsewhere in the municipality.

Services on Karmøy

  • Office cleaning: regular cleaning, as often as you need.
  • Specialist cleaning: when your premises need a more thorough round.
  • Floor stripping and polishing: for worn vinyl and linoleum floors.

Cleaning tailored to different buildings

Karmøy has a varied business community, with industry, retail and offices. Many buildings have storage, workshops, changing rooms or a canteen as well as offices. Cleaning is tailored to what is in the premises, so everything gets the right treatment.

Approved and local

R & E Renhold AS is approved by the Labour Inspection Authority and has been in the cleaning business since 2016. We also cover Haugesund, Tysvær, Bokn and Sveio.
