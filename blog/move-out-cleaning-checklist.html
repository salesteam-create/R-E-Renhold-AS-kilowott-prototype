Move-out cleaning checklist: room by room

Home and businessPublished 27 November 20263 min read

A move-out clean is the most thorough clean most people ever do. Whether you are leaving a flat or your business is moving out of an office, the next tenant or owner expects everything to be clean, including the places you normally never see. Use the checklist below as a reminder.

Before you start

A move-out clean goes fastest when the rooms are empty. Empty all cupboards and drawers, and take photos of the premises before you begin. The photos are useful if any questions come up at handover.

Have these ready: microfibre cloths, all-purpose cleaner, descaler, oven cleaner, a bucket, a mop, a vacuum cleaner and a safe step ladder.

Clean from top to bottom. Dust and dirt then fall onto surfaces you have not cleaned yet, so you do not have to do anything twice.

Kitchen

  • Clean all cupboards and drawers, inside and out.
  • Clean the oven, baking trays, racks and hob.
  • Pull out the cooker and fridge, and clean behind, underneath and at the sides.
  • Defrost and clean the fridge and freezer. Leave the doors open afterwards.
  • Clean the extractor fan and grease filter.
  • Descale the sink and tap, and wipe the tiles above the worktop.

Bathroom and laundry room

  • Descale tiles, shower screens and fittings.
  • Clean the drain and remove hair and soap residue.
  • Clean the toilet thoroughly, including around the base and behind.
  • Wipe mirrors, cabinets, lights and vents.
  • Clean the floor and the grout.

Living rooms, bedrooms and offices

  • Wipe doors, frames, skirting boards and sockets.
  • Clean the windows on the inside, including frames and sills.
  • Clean inside cupboards and wardrobes.
  • Dust lamps, radiators and high surfaces.
  • Vacuum and wash the floor, including corners and along the walls.

Commercial premises: often forgotten

When a business moves out of an office or shop, a few places are often forgotten:

  • Break rooms and kitchenettes, including the coffee machine and dishwasher.
  • Storage rooms, store cupboards and archives.
  • Ventilation vents and light fittings.
  • Signs, notices and tape on walls and doors.
  • Floor coverings in corridors and the entrance.

Worn vinyl and linoleum floors can often be brought back to life by stripping and re-polishing before handover. Read more in our guide to floor polishing and maintenance.

Handover

Norwegian tenancy law and most leases assume that premises are returned tidy and clean. Check what your contract says about the expected standard and any requirement for professional cleaning.

  • Walk through the premises together with the landlord or new owner.
  • Take new photos when the cleaning is finished.
  • Agree in writing if anything needs to be put right afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a move-out clean take?

It varies with the size and how dirty it is. Allow plenty of time: a typical flat often takes a full day for one person, while larger homes and premises take longer.

Who is responsible for the move-out clean?

Normally the person moving out. Check the lease or purchase agreement, which often states the standard expected.

Do the windows need cleaning on the outside?

Cleaning the inside is standard. Cleaning the outside of windows that cannot be reached safely is often agreed separately. Check what your contract says.
